Common Questions

Frequently Asked

What types of businesses do you work with?

We partner with retailers, wholesalers, distributors, institutional buyers, and businesses seeking reliable import/export or sourcing support. Our focus is strictly B2B.

Do you have minimum order quantities?

MOQs vary by product category and sourcing origin. We accommodate both large bulk orders and smaller wholesale quantities — please contact us with your specific requirements for a tailored response.

How quickly can you respond to sourcing requests?

For standard sourcing inquiries, we typically provide an initial response and preliminary pricing within 48–72 business hours. Complex or multi-category requests may take slightly longer.

Are you able to handle customs and documentation?

Yes. Our import/export service includes complete documentation support, customs clearance coordination, and compliance with all UAE trade regulations — end to end.
