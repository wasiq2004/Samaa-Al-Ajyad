Company Overview

A Trusted Name in
UAE General Trading

Samaa Al Ajyad General Trading L.L.C is a registered limited liability company headquartered in Dubai, United Arab Emirates. Established with a vision to deliver excellence across the general trading landscape, we operate at the intersection of global supply chains and UAE's dynamic commercial environment.

Our business spans multiple product categories — from garments and footwear to consumer goods and general merchandise — serving a diverse clientele of retailers, wholesalers, institutional buyers, and distribution partners.

Anchored in Dubai's world-class trade infrastructure, we leverage the UAE's strategic geographic advantage to facilitate seamless cross-border commerce across MENA, Asia, and beyond.
